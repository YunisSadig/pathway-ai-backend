Thank you for your interest in studying at Poznan University of Technology. We are pleased that you are considering our university as a place to continue your education and explore your passions.

The team at the International Relations Office can help you at every step of the application process for our Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes - from choosing the right field of study to completing the final formalities related to admission. Our goal is to make it easier for you to begin your studies and enter the academic world of Poznan University of Technology.

Questions about the admission process? We are here to help.
