Enrollment step by step

Do you want to study at Lodz University of Technology, but don't know where to start applying for admission? 
Here's a step-by-step look at the qualification process.

Step 1

Register on the admission portal

You can register at any time, starting from the date given in the schedule. Registration consists of free creation of an account in the recruitment portal.

 

Step 2

Choose degree and programmes

We offer 65 programmes in Polish, English and French. You can choose up to 4 programmes. There is a lot to choose from! If you feel a little dizzy, use the search engine for programmes.

Step 3

Enter your documents

Enter your high school diploma or equivalent school diploma results and attach scans of documents required in a given round and step of admission.

Step 4

Pay the enrollment fee

The enrollment fee for each course is PLN 85, in the case of architecture, design and industrial design engineering - PLN 150. You will make the payment through the recruitment portal.

Step 5

Check the lists of qualified for admission.

You will find lists of qualified applicants and reserve lists on the day the results are announced in the Results tab. If you get into more than one programme, you need to decide which programme you will study - for this programme you print the application from the recruitment portal, sign it and upload it again to the portal in the designated place. 

Step 6

Submit documents to the Incoming Students and Staff Mobility Division

You deliver a signed application printed from the recruitment portal and the rest of the application documents to the Incoming Students and Staff Mobility Division. What other documents do you need to submit? Check.

Step 7

You are accepted!

Congratulations! You're a student of the TUL. We will meet on campus in October.

Step 1

Register on the admission portal

You can register at any time, starting from the date given in the schedule. Registration consists of free creation of an account in the recruitment portal.

 

Step 2

Choose degree and programmes

We offer 55 programmes for second-degree studies in Polish and English. You can choose up to 4 programmes. There is a lot to choose from! If you feel a little dizzy, use the search engine for programmes.

Step 3

Enter the results

Enter the results of your first degree and attach scans of documents required in a given round and step of admission.

Step 4

Pay the enrollment fee

The enrollment fee for each programme is 85 PLN, for programmes with an entrance exam (Master of Business Studies and Smart Manufacturing) 100 PLN. You will make the payment through the recruitment portal.

Step 5

Check the lists of qualified for admission.

You will find lists of qualified applicants and reserve lists on the day the results are announced in the Results tab. If you are qualified for more than one programme, you need to decide which programme you will study - for this programme you print the application from the recruitment portal, sign it and upload it again to the portal in the designated email.

Step 6

Submit documents to the Incoming Students and Staff Mobility Division

You deliver a signed application printed from the recruitment portal and the rest of the application documents to the Incoming Students and Staff Mobility Division. What other documents do you need to submit? Check.

Step 7

You are accepted!

Congratulations! You're a student of TUL. We will meet you on campus soon.

In order to apply to the Technical University of Lodz by way of learning outcomes, the candidate sends a set of required documents to the unit chosen by him/her, which conducts the field of study he/she wants to study: Required documents | Rekrutacja - Politechnika Łódzka (lodz.pl).

The University Recruitment Committee creates a ranking list of candidates entitled to apply for admission to study by way of recognition of learning outcomes, who achieved the best results in the process of confirmation of learning outcomes.

The result obtained in the process of confirmation of learning outcomes is the value of the indicator, which is the sum of the product of the number of ECTS points and grades obtained as a result of recognition of learning outcomes for individual subjects.

Admission to studies through the process of recognition of learning outcomes takes place within the framework of a ranking list, until the number of places is exhausted.
