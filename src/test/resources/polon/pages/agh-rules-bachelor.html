General recruitment rules

  • Recruitment is carried out in recruitment cycles.
  • Fields of Bachelor (first-cycle) studies starting the education cycle in the 2026/2027 academic year, for which recruitment will be conducted, are included in the Resolution No 76/2025 of the AGH University Senate - Table 1.
  • Fields of study declared by the candidate cannot include the field of study in which the person is currently studying or completed at the same faculty of the AGH University as a part of the same level of study.
  • A candidate preliminarily qualified for several fields of study can register for only one field of study.

Admission rate

The basis for qualifications for all fields of study is an admission rate W, on the basis of which ranking lists of candidates are prepared.

The maximum value of the admission rate WR is 1000 points.

Only candidates who obtained the admission rate equal to or greater than 300 points may be admitted to the first year of full-time Bachelor (first-cycle) studies.

The results of the subjects from your education document will be used to calculate the admission rate for each declared field of study.

Based on the admission rate, you will be qualified for the declared fields of study.

Check what you need to know depending on the document you have:

You do not take the AGH math exam.

Enter results of the maturity certificate (świadectwo dojrzałości) and, if applicable, your vocation exam results into e-Rekrutacja system in the section “Grades”.

The candidate is responsible for the correctness of the results entered into the system and bears all the consequences of entering them incorrectly.

Depending on the certificate you have, enter the results as follows:

  • maturity exam –  percentage points from the maturity certificate in mathematics at the basic level and in other subjects at the advanced level,
  • a diploma confirming qualifications in a profession (2017 formula) – results from the written and practical sections obtained for all qualifications identified in a given profession, as displayed on the diploma confirming vocational qualifications,
  • a vocational diploma (2019 formula) – the final result from the vocational diploma.

E-Rekrutacja system will compare the entered results with the National Maturity Register (Krajowy Rejestr Matur), if they are available there. If there are any differences, you will receive a message.

If you are a laureate or finalist of an Olympiad that qualifies for exemption from the qualification procedure or for additional points, upload a scan of the certificate of obtaining the title of laureate or finalist to e-Rekrutacja system.

If you have a sport class (championship/international championship, first or second) or additional achievements, you can receive additional points added to your admission rate. For more information check the website Additional Points.

The candidate is responsible for the correctness of the results entered into the system and bears all the consequences of entering them incorrectly.

Depending on the certificate you have, enter the results in the following way:

  • European Baccalaureate – grades on a scale of 0-10 in mathematics at the basic level (2-3 hours per week) and in other subjects at the advanced level (4-5 hours per week). If you took mathematics at the advanced level (4-5 hours per week), the advanced result will be included in the M component,
  • International Baccalaureate – grades on a scale of 1-7 in mathematics at the SL level and in other subjects at the HL level. If you took Mathematics at HL level, the HL result will be included in the M component.

If you have a sport class (championship/international championship, first or second) or additional achievements, you can receive additional points added to your admission rate. For more information check the website Additional Points.

  • Polish maturity certificate (świadectwo dojrzałości) or
  • IB diploma (International Baccalaureate) or
  • EB diploma (European Baccalaureate).
  • Your certificate was issued by a school or educational institution operating in the education system of one of the EU, EFTA and OECD countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Portugal, Republic of Korea, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Kingdom, United States, Hungary, Italy, as well as Ukraine and China (gaozhong secondary school leaving certificate 高中毕业证书) and

Language document

If you have a Polish secondary school leaving certificate, you do not need to submit a language certificate, regardless of your citizenship.

If you have the foreign secondary school leaving certificate, check whether the language document is required.

  • You do not need to provide the language certificate if you hold a secondary school leaving certificate issued by a school or educational institution operating within the education system of one of the following EU, EFTA, and OECD countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Portugal, Republic of Korea, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, United Kingdom, United States, Ukraine and China (gaozhong secondary school leaving certificate 高中毕业证书), IB diploma, or EB diploma.
  • You must provide a language certificate if you hold the secondary school leaving certificate not listed above. Check the list of required language documents in the section Required documents for Bachelor studies.
