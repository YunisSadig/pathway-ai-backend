Second-cycle (M.Sc.) studies - Fees for international students


RECRUITMENT FEE FOR THE ACADEMIC YEAR 2026/2027

150 PLN - Architecture

100 PLN - other programmes, for Candidates required to take the entrance exam

85 PLN - other programmes, for Candidates not required to take an entrance exam

The candidate pays a single fee, regardless of the number of selected programmes. If you choose programmes with different fees, you should pay the higher fee. The fee is non-refundable.

Refund policy


In accordance with applicable Ordinance 18 of the Rector of Poznan University of Technology regarding the tuition fee refund process, the following rules apply:

  1. Failure to commence studies
    If an international student fails to commence their studies, they are entitled to a refund of the tuition fee paid, after deducting the cost of the refund transaction.

  2. Refund deadlines
    The University will process the refund within one month from the date of receiving a written refund request.

  3. The application fee is non-refundable.
