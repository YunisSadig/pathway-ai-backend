AI-native Software, Computing Sciences and Electrical Engineering

Join us in creating the AI-powered digital world

Software is no longer static. It learns, adapts, and collaborates with humans. Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming not only what software does, but how it is designed, built, and evolved, shaping new ways of engineering software systems throughout their lifecycle. The AI‑Native Software specialization prepares you to lead this transformation. You will learn to create software systems where AI is not an add-on, but a fundamental building block, powering intelligent behaviour, continuous improvement, and new kinds of user experiences. Come study with us and shape the future of software in an AI-powered world.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion of the AI-native Software specialisation, you will understand AI-native software engineering and be able to design, develop, and improve high-quality software systems where AI is an integral part of the solution. You will be able to 

  • Develop and evaluate demanding AI-native software systems 

  • Apply AI-enabled methods and tools throughout software development 

  • Assess architectures, technologies, and trade-offs in AI-native systems 

  • Consider ethical, legal, security, and sustainability aspects 

  • Follow advances in software engineering and AI, supporting expert work, research, and doctoral studies.
