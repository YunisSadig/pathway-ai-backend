Bachelor’s Degree Programme in Immersive Software Engineering and AI

Launch your career during your studies in Finland

No one becomes an IT professional by chance – it requires dedication and consistent effort. That’s why this programme mirrors real working life. Instead of memorisation or traditional exams, the learning process emphasises exploring concepts through meaningful activities. Your progress is tracked weekly, and you take ownership of your development building skills systematically.

Software engineering combines logical reasoning with systematic problem-solving. AI's role in coding is growing but it isn’t a shortcut to success. Ultimately, those who succeed are the ones with the skills to create software and effectively leverage AI as a tool in the process.

Career opportunities for ISE&AI graduates 

Completing the programme equips you with the expertise to thrive in diverse professional environments. Graduates are prepared for a wide range of career paths, including:

  • R&D Engineer – Innovating and prototyping next-generation technologies.
  • Software Developer or Architect – Designing complex, scalable systems for critical applications.
  • Entrepreneur – Launching your own high-tech startup or pursuing an innovation-driven career.
  • Technology Consultant or Product Manager – Leading digital transformation and product innovation across industries.
  • Academic Researcher – Advancing to master’s or doctoral studies in scientific and technological fields.

Testimonials

Why choose Finland and Jyväskylä?

Finland is one of the world’s top destinations for international students, offering high-quality education in a safe, inclusive, and forward-thinking society. Finland provides a fair and supportive environment for building a career—and is especially known for enabling women to successfully balance work and family life. Ranked the happiest country in the world for eight consecutive years (UN World Happiness Report), Finland combines academic excellence with an exceptional quality of life.

Get to know the Faculty of Information Technology

The faculty's teaching and research span a broad spectrum of the IT field, ranging from technical and mathematical challenges to system management and organisational practices. Software engineering and artificial intelligence are among the faculty’s core areas of expertise.

With an international staff and active collaboration with the IT industry, the faculty maintains a strong connection between academic excellence and real-world's needs and challenges.
