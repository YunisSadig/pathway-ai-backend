Bachelor's Degree Programme in Business Innovation and Sustainability

Why this programme?

By attending the Bachelor of Business Innovation and Sustainability programme you will learn science-based thinking and logic in business and sustainability. Our courses support business skills to create new knowledge.  You will also get the benefits of a small programme: Our courses take place in smaller groups where you get to interact directly with teachers and other students. You will get personal counseling and personal study plan individually tailored for your career purposes.

In our programme in Business Innovation and Sustainability, you will learn about the management of business innovations, sustainable management, new business creation, decision-making, and sustainability transition.

This programme also offers high-quality elective studies in digital marketing, responsible management, and entrepreneurship, among others. In addition, we provide multiple opportunities for student exchange and trainee periods in companies.

Continue your study path to a master’s degree 

Did you know? The bachelor programme offers you a gateway to JSBE’s well-known and popular master’s programmes (M.Sc.). This option – after the bachelor level graduation – enables you even more career opportunities and new academic research skills and pathway to study at the international M.Sc. programmes at the University of Jyväskylä School of Business and Economics.    

Student life in Jyväskylä

Meet new people and network with your colleague students. The modern environment in the active city of Jyväskylä supports your learning.

Jyväskylä is a vibrant and lively campus town with a welcoming atmosphere for international students. At the Jyväskylä School of Business and Economics, Pörssi Student Association offers you multiple opportunities to network with similar-minded students and to meet new people.  

Career opportunities

By graduating at the bachelor's degree level, you can work at multiple specialist and management positions in firms and public organizations. Typical entry level jobs relate to consulting, corporate social responsibility, sustainable business, business process management, customer interface, and sales.     

Double your career opportunities by getting international business diploma! At the same time have great fun with learning new skills needed for a business career development.

Jyväskylä University School of Business and Economics

The Jyväskylä University School of Business and Economics (JSBE) is an AACSB-accredited business school and one of the six faculties of the University of Jyväskylä. As a part of a university internationally renown for high-quality research and innovativeness in learning and teaching development, we strive for excellence in research and research-based teaching in the key fields of economic sciences.

Our disciplines are Accounting, Commercial Law, Corporate Communication, Corporate Environmental Management, Economics, Management and Leadership, Marketing, and Strategy and Entrepreneurship.
