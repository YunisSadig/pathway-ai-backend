Programme at a glance

  • In this programme, you will gain relevant skills to design and manage digitally evolving businesses in a sustainable and responsible way.
  • You will explore how constantly evolving digital technologies are transforming business and enabling new ways to create value.
  • You will get familiar with the logic of platform and data economies and digital ecosystems.
  • You will acquire the essential skills needed to develop and scale digital services in a global context.
  • After completing the programme, you can continue your studies directly in one of the business master’s programmes at LUT.

Continue your studies in a master's programme

After completing a bachelor's degree, you can continue your studies in one of LUT’s master's programmes in business. Each programme is 120 ECTS credits, and you will graduate within two academic years.

Programmes offered in English:

  • Master's Programme in International Business and Entrepreneurship, Lappeenranta campus
  • Master's Programme in International Marketing Management, Lappeenranta campus
  • Master's Programme in Supply Management, Lappeenranta campus
  • Master's Programme in Business Analytics (for those, who have completed minor studies in Software and Information Systems) 
  • A new master's programme in strategic management, Lahti campus (programme starts in 2029)

You can also apply separately to the Finnish-language master's programmes: Strateginen myynti or Tietojohtaminen ja johtajuus.
