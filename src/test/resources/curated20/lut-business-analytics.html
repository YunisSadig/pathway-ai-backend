Who is this programme for? 

This programme is for you if you want to make an impact on business decisions by harnessing the value of data. Learn to channel and combine your passion for data analysis, programming, and business to create competitive advantages and data-driven insights.  

 

In addition, you have 

 

  • a bachelor’s degree in business administration, applied mathematics, IT or computer science, engineering, statistics, management science, OR decision sciences and similar data-related areas;
  • an interest in combining mathematics, operations research, computer science or IT into business solutions;
  • skills in problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, and pattern recognition in data.

What will you learn in the programme? 

As a graduate, you will be a competent business analytics professional who knows and can critically analyse the theories and methods in the field.

During the programme, you will acquire:

  • professional skills to critically analyse the theories and methods in this field; you will be able to choose the correct business analytics methods and use them in practice to draw logical conclusions and make recommendations in strategic decision-making situations;
  • competence in applied business research; you will be able to draw justified conclusions and analytically discuss the implications of research results;
  • responsible business skills to critically evaluate consequences of business decisions from a responsibility perspective;
  • teamwork skills: you will have good collaboration and interpersonal communication skills and will be an effective team member and capable of solving problems in teams.
