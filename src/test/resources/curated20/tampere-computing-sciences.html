Computing Sciences and Electrical Engineering

Contribute to the future as an expert in computing sciences and electrical engineering

Our interdisciplinary degree programme allows you to keep up with the rapidly evolving technological landscape while also contributing to the advancement of scientific knowledge. Focusing on Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, or Computational Data Analysis, you will learn to lead technological innovations and solve industrial challenges.

Find out more about studying computing sciences and electrical engineering!

Computing Sciences and Electrical Engineering combines software, electronics, AI, and communications to solve real-world challenges. Through hands-on projects and laboratory work, you'll gain the skills needed for careers in fields such as embedded systems, machine learning, telecommunications, renewable energy, and next-generation digital technologies.
